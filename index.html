Universal Email Client Setup

Step-by-step instructions for pasting your high-definition signature into any major email client.

$ cat docs/installation-matrix.md
100% SUCCESS RATE
01
Click the "Copy Rich Signature" button in the MailCraft Studio above.
02
Open Gmail in your web browser and click the Settings (gear icon) > See all settings.
03
Scroll down to the Signature section and click + Create new.
04
Click inside the signature textarea and press Cmd + V (Mac) or Ctrl + V (Windows) to paste.
05
Under Signature defaults, select your new signature for new emails and replies, then click Save Changes at the bottom.
// GMAIL RETINA OPTIMIZATION: High-definition avatars render with subpixel smoothness and crisp vector icons automatically.
01
Click "Copy Rich Signature" in the Studio.
02
Open Apple Mail on your Mac and go to Mail > Settings (or Preferences) in the menu bar.
03
Select the Signatures tab, click the + button, and uncheck "Always match my default message font".
04
Click into the edit pane on the right, delete existing text, and press Cmd + V to paste.
// APPLE MAIL NOTE: Apple Mail preserves Retina image scaling and vector SVG icons without compression.
01
Click "Copy Rich Signature" in the Studio.
02
In Outlook Web, click Settings (gear) > Mail > Compose and reply > + New signature.
03
Paste the signature into the editor with Ctrl + V or Cmd + V, then click Save.
// OUTLOOK MSO FIXES: Embedded conditional comments ensure Microsoft Word and Outlook rendering engines maintain fixed column widths.
01
Click "Copy HTML Source" in the Studio.
02
In Thunderbird, open Account Settings, check "Use HTML", and paste the code into the signature box.
