1. WHAT ARE COOKIES & LOCAL STORAGE?

Cookies and HTML5 LocalStorage are standard web browser technologies that allow applications to remember state and data on your device across browsing sessions without requiring server logins.

2. WHAT STORAGE WE USE & WHY

MailCraft Studio uses zero advertising cookies and zero tracking cookies. We exclusively utilize mandatory functional LocalStorage keys:

  • mailcraft_state: Stores your active signature draft (name, job title, phone, email, color selections, quotes, and avatar).
  • mailcraft_user_presets: Stores your saved named custom presets created in the Preset Manager.
  • mailcraft_cookie_consent: Stores your acknowledgment of this client storage policy.
⚠ CRITICAL WARNING: WHAT HAPPENS IF YOU DELETE COOKIES OR LOCAL STORAGE?
Because MailCraft Studio does not utilize any cloud databases or user accounts, your browser's LocalStorage is the sole repository of your saved custom presets and draft edits.

If you clear your browser cookies, site cache, or browser history, all your custom presets and draft signature modifications will be permanently deleted and reset back to initial factory defaults.
3. HOW TO SAFEGUARD YOUR SIGNATURES

To ensure your custom configurations are never lost when clearing browser data, open the Preset Manager (presets --manage) in Studio and click [Export JSON]. This saves a portable backup file to your computer that you can re-import anytime with 1 click.
